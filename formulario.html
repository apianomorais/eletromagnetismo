Formulário

Tudo o que o curso usa, em uma página só: operadores, teoremas, as equações de Maxwell nas quatro formas e as condições de contorno.

1. Operadores nas três coordenadas

Nas três linhas abaixo, \(T\) é um campo escalar e \(\vet{v}\) um campo vetorial. Em cilíndricas, \(s\) é a distância ao eixo \(z\); em esféricas, \(r\) é a distância à origem, \(\theta\) o ângulo polar medido do eixo \(z\) e \(\phi\) o azimutal.

Cartesianas

\[ \nabla T = \pder{T}{x}\vers{x} + \pder{T}{y}\vers{y} + \pder{T}{z}\vers{z} \] \[ \divg{\vet{v}} = \pder{v_x}{x} + \pder{v_y}{y} + \pder{v_z}{z} \] \[ \rot{\vet{v}} = \left(\pder{v_z}{y}-\pder{v_y}{z}\right)\vers{x} + \left(\pder{v_x}{z}-\pder{v_z}{x}\right)\vers{y} + \left(\pder{v_y}{x}-\pder{v_x}{y}\right)\vers{z} \] \[ \nabla^2 T = \frac{\partial^2T}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2T}{\partial y^2} + \frac{\partial^2T}{\partial z^2}, \qquad d\tau = dx\,dy\,dz \]

Cilíndricas

\[ \nabla T = \pder{T}{s}\vers{s} + \frac{1}{s}\pder{T}{\phi}\vers{\phi} + \pder{T}{z}\vers{z} \] \[ \divg{\vet{v}} = \frac{1}{s}\pder{(sv_s)}{s} + \frac{1}{s}\pder{v_\phi}{\phi} + \pder{v_z}{z} \] \[ \rot{\vet{v}} = \left(\frac{1}{s}\pder{v_z}{\phi}-\pder{v_\phi}{z}\right)\vers{s} + \left(\pder{v_s}{z}-\pder{v_z}{s}\right)\vers{\phi} + \frac{1}{s}\left(\pder{(sv_\phi)}{s}-\pder{v_s}{\phi}\right)\vers{z} \] \[ \nabla^2 T = \frac{1}{s}\pder{}{s}\left(s\pder{T}{s}\right) + \frac{1}{s^2}\frac{\partial^2T}{\partial\phi^2} + \frac{\partial^2T}{\partial z^2}, \qquad d\tau = s\,ds\,d\phi\,dz \]

Esféricas

\[ \nabla T = \pder{T}{r}\vers{r} + \frac{1}{r}\pder{T}{\theta}\vers{\theta} + \frac{1}{r\sen\theta}\pder{T}{\phi}\vers{\phi} \] \[ \divg{\vet{v}} = \frac{1}{r^2}\pder{(r^2v_r)}{r} + \frac{1}{r\sen\theta}\pder{(\sen\theta\,v_\theta)}{\theta} + \frac{1}{r\sen\theta}\pder{v_\phi}{\phi} \] \[ \nabla^2 T = \frac{1}{r^2}\pder{}{r}\left(r^2\pder{T}{r}\right) + \frac{1}{r^2\sen\theta}\pder{}{\theta} \left(\sen\theta\pder{T}{\theta}\right) + \frac{1}{r^2\sen^2\theta}\frac{\partial^2T}{\partial\phi^2} \] \[ \rot{\vet{v}} = \frac{1}{r\sen\theta} \left[\pder{(\sen\theta\,v_\phi)}{\theta}-\pder{v_\theta}{\phi}\right]\vers{r} + \frac{1}{r}\left[\frac{1}{\sen\theta}\pder{v_r}{\phi} -\pder{(rv_\phi)}{r}\right]\vers{\theta} + \frac{1}{r}\left[\pder{(rv_\theta)}{r}-\pder{v_r}{\theta}\right]\vers{\phi} \] \[ d\tau = r^2\sen\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi, \qquad d\vet{a}\big|_{r=R} = R^2\sen\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi\;\vers{r} \]

2. Identidades, teoremas e a delta

Identidades que o curso usa

\[ \rot{\left(\nabla T\right)} = 0, \qquad \divg{\left(\rot{\vet{v}}\right)} = 0 \] \[ \rot{\left(\rot{\vet{v}}\right)} = \nabla\left(\divg{\vet{v}}\right) - \nabla^2\vet{v} \] \[ \nabla(fg) = f\nabla g + g\nabla f, \qquad \divg{(f\vet{A})} = f\divg{\vet{A}} + \vet{A}\cdot\nabla f \] \[ \rot{(f\vet{A})} = f\rot{\vet{A}} - \vet{A}\cruz\nabla f \] \[ \divg{\left(\vet{A}\cruz\vet{B}\right)} = \vet{B}\cdot\left(\rot{\vet{A}}\right) - \vet{A}\cdot\left(\rot{\vet{B}}\right) \] \[ \vet{A}\cruz\left(\vet{B}\cruz\vet{C}\right) = \vet{B}\left(\vet{A}\cdot\vet{C}\right) - \vet{C}\left(\vet{A}\cdot\vet{B}\right) \]

Teoremas integrais

\[ \int_a^b\left(\nabla T\right)\cdot d\vet{\ell} = T(b)-T(a) \] \[ \int_V\left(\divg{\vet{v}}\right)d\tau = \oint_S \vet{v}\cdot d\vet{a} \] \[ \int_S\left(\rot{\vet{v}}\right)\cdot d\vet{a} = \oint_C \vet{v}\cdot d\vet{\ell} \] \[ \int_V f\left(\divg{\vet{A}}\right)d\tau = -\int_V \vet{A}\cdot\left(\nabla f\right)d\tau + \oint_S f\vet{A}\cdot d\vet{a} \]

A delta de Dirac

\[ \int f(x)\,\delta(x-a)\,dx = f(a) \quad\text{se } a \text{ está no intervalo; zero se não está} \] \[ \divg{\left(\frac{\vers{r}}{r^2}\right)} = 4\pi\delta^3(\vet{r}), \qquad \nabla^2\!\left(\frac{1}{r}\right) = -4\pi\delta^3(\vet{r}) \] \[ \delta(kx) = \frac{1}{|k|}\delta(x), \qquad \delta\big(g(x)\big) = \sum_i\frac{\delta(x-x_i)}{|g'(x_i)|} \]

Funções e expansões

\[ \frac{1}{\left|\vet{r}-\vet{r}'\right|} = \sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\frac{r_<^{\,l}}{r_>^{\,l+1}}\,P_l(\cos\gamma), \qquad \int_{-1}^{1}P_l P_{l'}\,du = \frac{2}{2l+1}\delta_{ll'} \] \[ P_0 = 1,\quad P_1 = u,\quad P_2 = \tfrac12(3u^2-1), \quad P_3 = \tfrac12(5u^3-3u) \]

O padrão vale a pena guardar: carga superficial faz saltar a componente perpendicular; corrente superficial faz saltar a paralela.

4. Campos, potenciais e energia

Eletrostática

\[ \vet{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\int \frac{\vers{\imath}}{\imath^2}\,\rho\,d\tau', \qquad V = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\int\frac{\rho}{\imath}\,d\tau' \] \[ \vet{E} = -\nabla V, \qquad \nabla^2 V = -\frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0} \] \[ W = \frac{\varepsilon_0}{2}\int E^2 d\tau, \qquad u_E = \frac{\varepsilon_0E^2}{2} \quad\left(\frac{\vet{D}\cdot\vet{E}}{2}\text{ na matéria linear}\right) \] \[ V(\vet{r}) = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\sum_{n} \frac{1}{r^{n+1}}\int (r')^nP_n(\cos\alpha)\,\rho\,d\tau' \qquad\text{(multipolos)} \] \[ \vet{p} = \int\vet{r}'\rho\,d\tau',\qquad V_{\text{dip}} = \frac{\vet{p}\cdot\vers{r}}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2} \]

Magnetostática

\[ \vet{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}I\int\frac{d\vet{\ell}'\cruz\vers{\imath}} {\imath^2}, \qquad \vet{A} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int\frac{\vet{J}}{\imath}\,d\tau' \] \[ \vet{B} = \rot{\vet{A}}, \qquad \nabla^2\vet{A} = -\mu_0\vet{J} \quad\text{(calibre de Coulomb)} \] \[ W = \frac{1}{2\mu_0}\int B^2 d\tau, \qquad u_B = \frac{B^2}{2\mu_0} \] \[ \vet{m} = I\vet{a},\qquad \vet{\tau} = \vet{m}\cruz\vet{B},\qquad U = -\vet{m}\cdot\vet{B},\qquad \vet{F} = \nabla\left(\vet{m}\cdot\vet{B}\right) \]
